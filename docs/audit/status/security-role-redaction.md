# Status update - technician role boundary and customer financial redaction

- **AUTH-015 (warranty access):** the technician blocklist used `/api/warranty` while the API route is `/api/warranties`, so technicians could read and mutate warranty records. Both spellings are now blocked in `src/lib/role-access.ts`, and the warranty routes require `ADMIN`/`DISPATCHER` themselves.
- **AUTH-015 (customer, vehicle and job redaction):** customer list/detail responses returned invoice history, invoice counts, lifetime revenue, average ticket, and CLV to technicians; vehicle detail returned each job's quote and invoice totals; and assigned-job detail returned quote/invoice line items, payments, and parts cost/markup/vendor data. Those fields are now limited to office roles, and technicians receive only the work-order view.
- **AUTH-016 (portal and credential secrets):** customer `portalToken`, password hashes, TOTP secrets, raw and hashed reset tokens, verification tokens, session tokens, and OAuth access/refresh/ID tokens are stripped from every `apiSuccess()` response by `src/lib/api-redact.ts`. Dates and Prisma `Decimal` instances are preserved for normal JSON serialization.
- **Tests:** `src/__tests__/role-access.test.ts` covers the warranty route spellings, the technician job/settings allowlist, customer financial roles, and the customer API allowlist. `src/__tests__/api-redact.test.ts` covers nested portal-token removal and preservation of portal URLs, TOTP setup secrets, and Decimal-like values.
- **Verification:** type-check, lint, 112/112 Vitest tests, and the 141-page production build pass.
